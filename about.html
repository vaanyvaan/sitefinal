About

My name is Yvan Chirade.
I have always been passionate about everything related to graphic art, especially drawing, and art in general. I have been drawing since I was very young. I am also very sensitive to all forms of visual creation. Over time, and as I began my art studies, I developed a strong interest in audiovisual media in all its forms.

My graphic universe is strongly oriented towards the bizarre, metamorphosed bodies and the spiritual, all with a fantastical touch. The gestures, the fluidity of the silhouettes and their empty gaze touch me deeply.
